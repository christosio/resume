Boccia

BC1 Boccia Athlete

In addition to my work in web and UI/UX development, I am also a dedicated **Boccia athlete**, competing in the **BC1 classification**. Boccia is a precision sport designed for athletes with severe physical disabilities and is a recognized Paralympic sport.

I proudly represented my skills at the **Astana Challenger 2025**, an international competition that brought together top Boccia players from around the world. Competing at this level has strengthened my focus, strategy, and determination—qualities I bring into both sport and design.
